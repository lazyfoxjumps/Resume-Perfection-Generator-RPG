# Role Priors (Boss Stat Fallback)

Use this when the JD is under ~100 words, vague, or missing key signals. Pull the base profile by role family, then adjust up or down by any explicit signals the JD does include.

Format: STR / DEX / CON / INT / WIS / CHA.

---

## Software Engineer (IC, mid-level)

**Profile**: 12 / 13 / 12 / 15 / 11 / 9
**Common boss buffs**: cares about systems thinking (INT), code review quality (WIS).
**Common boss debuffs**: tolerates low CHA, low public-facing work.

## Adjustment Rules

After picking the base profile, scan the JD for these signals and adjust:

- "Lead a team of N" → +1 to +3 STR and WIS depending on N
- "5+ years" → CON floor of 13
- "10+ years" → CON floor of 16
- "Deep expertise in X" → +2 INT
- "Public speaking / external presence" → +2 CHA
- "Fast-paced, ambiguous" → +2 DEX
- "Highly regulated" → +2 CON and WIS
- "Mission-driven" → +1 WIS, prefer Paladin signals
