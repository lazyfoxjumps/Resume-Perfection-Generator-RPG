# Classes

Eight base classes. Multi-class when the runner-up scores within 15% of the leader. Cap at two classes. If no class clears at least 3 signal matches, label the adventurer as "Wandering {closest class}."

For each class: archetype, signal phrases (search resume for these), dominant stats, typical roles.

---

## Warrior

**Archetype**: Operators and executors. They ship, they hold the line, they own delivery. Direct, results-first, hands dirty.

**Signal phrases**: "led delivery," "shipped," "owned end-to-end," "scaled team," "operations," "production," "on-call," "incident response," "P&L," "managed N reports," "fulfillment," "logistics," "throughput."

**Dominant stats**: STR, CON.

**Typical roles**: Engineering manager, ops lead, COO, head of delivery, program manager, plant manager, supply chain lead.

---

## Mage

**Archetype**: Researchers and strategists. They think deeply, they model the world, they bring rigor. Often the smartest person in the room and quietly so.

**Signal phrases**: "research," "published," "modeled," "analysis," "hypothesis," "experiment," "framework," "thesis," "whitepaper," "PhD," "literature review," "RCT," "statistical," "peer-reviewed."

**Dominant stats**: INT, WIS.

**Typical roles**: Research scientist, strategy consultant, data scientist (research-leaning), policy analyst, quant, academic, principal engineer (R&D).

---

## Rogue

**Archetype**: Hunters and closers. They find the angle, they move fast, they win where others stall. Sales, growth, dealmaking, pivots.

**Signal phrases**: "exceeded quota," "closed," "growth," "pipeline," "ARR," "BD," "partnerships," "negotiated," "ramped," "scaled revenue," "0-to-1," "founding," "deal," "GTM."

**Dominant stats**: DEX, CHA.

**Typical roles**: AE, head of sales, BD lead, growth lead, founder, partnerships manager, biz dev.

---

## Bard

**Archetype**: Storytellers and signal-makers. They shape narrative, they build brands, they make people feel things. Marketing, content, comms, design that sells.

**Signal phrases**: "brand," "content," "campaign," "voice," "narrative," "comms," "PR," "social," "engagement," "audience," "creative direction," "copywriting," "rebrand," "thought leadership."

**Dominant stats**: CHA, DEX.

**Typical roles**: Marketing lead, head of content, brand strategist, creative director, comms lead, social media manager.

---

## Cleric

**Archetype**: Caretakers and culture-builders. They tend the people side. HR, support, ops, coaching, the glue work that keeps teams alive.

**Signal phrases**: "people ops," "HR," "DEI," "employee experience," "onboarding," "training," "L&D," "coaching," "mentored," "support," "community management," "wellness," "engagement surveys."

**Dominant stats**: WIS, CHA.

**Typical roles**: People ops, HRBP, head of culture, support lead, community manager, executive coach, organizational psychologist.

---

## Ranger

**Archetype**: Generalists and full-stack survivors. They wear many hats, they cover wide territory, they adapt. Startup early-hires, solo operators, fractionals.

**Signal phrases**: "wore multiple hats," "full-stack," "founding team," "solo," "fractional," "freelance," "consulting," "across functions," "from scratch," "0-to-1," "set up," "built and ran."

**Dominant stats**: DEX, CON.

**Typical roles**: Founding engineer, chief of staff, fractional exec, solo consultant, head of {anything at a 10-person company}.

---

## Artificer

**Archetype**: Builders and tinkerers. Deep technical craft. They make the thing work, they know the stack inside out, they care about quality.

**Signal phrases**: "architecture," "infrastructure," "systems design," "performance," "refactor," "optimization," "stack," "framework," "open source," "contributor," "kernel," "compiler," "shipped library," "patent."

**Dominant stats**: INT, STR.

**Typical roles**: Senior/staff/principal engineer, systems architect, ML engineer, platform engineer, hardware engineer, technical lead.

---

## Paladin

**Archetype**: Mission-driven leaders. Values-first, purpose-led, often in impact-aligned roles. They organize people around a cause.

**Signal phrases**: "mission," "impact," "nonprofit," "social good," "sustainability," "climate," "advocacy," "ESG," "public sector," "civic," "movement," "values-aligned," "ethics," "responsible AI."

**Dominant stats**: WIS, CHA.

**Typical roles**: Nonprofit ED, head of impact, sustainability lead, civic tech lead, ethics officer, public-sector director.

---

## Multi-Class Examples

- **Bard / Artificer**: developer advocate, technical writer, head of devrel.
- **Rogue / Ranger**: founder of a 5-person seed-stage startup.
- **Mage / Paladin**: policy researcher at a think tank.
- **Cleric / Warrior**: head of people at a fast-scaling company.
- **Artificer / Mage**: ML research engineer, applied scientist.
