# Quest Templates

Base catalog of side and main quests. Pull from here, then sharpen with WebSearch for current, role-specific advice when the JD names skills or trends not covered below.

Each quest: name, tier (Side / Main), effort, primary stat gain, secondary stat gain, prereqs, age fit.

---

## STR Quests

### Add Hard Metrics to Three Bullets (Side)
- **Effort**: 1-2 hours
- **Primary gain**: STR +1
- **Secondary**: CHA +0.5
- **Prereqs**: access to old metrics, OKRs, or rough estimates
- **Age fit**: all stages

### Quantify Headcount and Scope on Every Role (Side)
- **Effort**: 1 hour
- **Primary gain**: STR +1
- **Prereqs**: memory of team sizes
- **Age fit**: all stages

### Lead a Visible End-to-End Project (Main)
- **Effort**: 3-6 months
- **Primary gain**: STR +3
- **Secondary**: WIS +1, CHA +1
- **Prereqs**: current role, manager buy-in
- **Age fit**: journeyman+

### Run a P&L or Own a Budget (Main)
- **Effort**: ongoing, 6+ months to show on resume
- **Primary gain**: STR +3, WIS +2
- **Prereqs**: senior IC or manager role
- **Age fit**: veteran+

## When to WebSearch

Pull current advice when:
- The JD mentions a tool, cert, or framework not in this catalog
- The role is in a fast-moving field (AI, crypto, climate tech, etc.)
- The adventurer is targeting a 2026 hiring market and you want current "what hiring managers care about" signals
- A quest's effort estimate would be wrong without current context (e.g., a cert that was hard 5 years ago is now table stakes)
