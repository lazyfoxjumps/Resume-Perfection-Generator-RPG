# Quest Templates

Base catalog of side, main, and epic quests. Pull from here, then sharpen with WebSearch for current, role-specific advice when the JD names skills or trends not covered below.

Each quest: name, tier (Side / Main / Epic), effort (with adventurer-hours estimate in parens), primary stat gain, secondary stat gain, **XP**, prereqs, age fit.

**XP formula** (see `references/xp-math.md`): `XP = (sum of primary + secondary stat gains) × 50 × difficulty_multiplier`. Multipliers: Side 1.0, Main 2.0, Epic 3.5. Adventurer-hours follow the part-time rule of thumb: 1 month ≈ 24 focused hours, unless the quest is a full-time role change.

## When to WebSearch

Pull current advice when:
- The JD mentions a tool, cert, or framework not in this catalog
- The role is in a fast-moving field (AI, crypto, climate tech, etc.)
- The adventurer is targeting a 2026 hiring market and you want current "what hiring managers care about" signals
- A quest's effort estimate would be wrong without current context (e.g., a cert that was hard 5 years ago is now table stakes)
- The adventurer is in **Professional Development Arc** mode (Path B) and needs goal-aligned quests not covered above. WebSearch for "skills for {target role} in 2026" and adapt findings into named quests with computed XP.
