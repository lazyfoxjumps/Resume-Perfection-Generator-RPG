# Stat Rubric

All six stats are scored 1-20. Use the anchored examples to calibrate. After raw scoring, apply age calibration (see SKILL.md "Age Calibration" section).

Modifiers (D&D-style) shown for chat flavor:
- Score 1-3: -4 (newborn)
- Score 4-7: -2 to -1 (apprentice)
- Score 8-12: 0 to +1 (competent)
- Score 13-15: +2 (skilled)
- Score 16-18: +3 to +4 (rare)
- Score 19-20: +5 (legendary)

---

## STR (Execution Power)

What it measures: how much the adventurer ships, how big the scope, who they move with them.

Signals that raise STR:
- Quantified shipping volume ("shipped 12 features," "delivered $4M in revenue," "served 80M users")
- Headcount managed
- Revenue, ARR, user, or throughput numbers
- Production ownership ("owned X end-to-end")
- Scope ("led delivery across 3 product lines")

Signals that lower STR:
- Vague duties ("responsible for," "assisted with") with no outcomes
- No quantified results across multiple roles

Anchors:
- 5: Junior IC with one shipped feature mentioned vaguely.
- 10: Competent mid-level with 2-3 clear shipped outcomes.
- 15: Strong senior owning end-to-end delivery with revenue/scale numbers.
- 20: Director/VP with multi-team scope and clear, large quantified impact.

## DEX (Tactical Agility)

What it measures: speed of iteration, tool range, ability to pivot.

Signals that raise DEX:
- Wide stack or tool variety
- Side projects, hackathons, open source
- Fast iteration evidence ("shipped weekly," "ran 40 experiments")
- Career pivots executed cleanly
- 0-to-1 work

Signals that lower DEX:
- Single stack for many years with no cross-training
- Slow-moving environments only (no fast-iteration signals)

Anchors:
- 5: One stack, one tool, no side work.
- 10: Two or three tools, some breadth.
- 15: Cross-stack, shipping fast, side projects or open source.
- 20: Rare polymath. Multiple deep stacks, 0-to-1 across domains, fast everywhere.

## CON (Endurance)

What it measures: tenure depth, sustained delivery, resilience.

Signals that raise CON:
- Long tenures (3+ years per role, ideally)
- Sustained multi-year projects
- On-call, incident response, turnaround stories
- Survived org transitions

Signals that lower CON:
- Job-hopping with no intentional pattern (under 18 months per role across several jobs)
- No long projects

Anchors:
- 5: All roles under 1 year, no long projects.
- 10: Average 2-year tenures, one sustained project.
- 15: Multiple 3+ year tenures, evidence of sustained ownership.
- 20: 5+ year tenures with deepening scope; turnaround or crisis leadership.

Note: short tenures from layoffs, startup failures, or intentional fractional work are not low-CON signals. Read context.

---

## INT (Technical Depth)

What it measures: domain mastery, technical rigor, formal expertise.

Signals that raise INT:
- Certifications relevant to field
- Advanced tooling, deep stack
- Research output, patents, papers
- Formal education in field (degree, bootcamp, residency)
- Specialized knowledge ("ML systems," "tax law," "cardiology")

Signals that lower INT:
- Stale tools (5+ years out of date for the target role)
- No depth in any one domain

Anchors:
- 5: Generalist with no formal depth.
- 10: Solid working knowledge in main domain.
- 15: Strong specialist with certs or proven depth.
- 20: World-class. PhD or principal-level + recognized output.

---

## WIS (Judgment)

What it measures: strategic thinking, tradeoff framing, knowing what NOT to do.

Signals that raise WIS:
- Strategy work, vision setting
- Decisions framed with explicit tradeoffs
- Mentorship of others
- Postmortems, retrospectives, lessons-learned authoring
- Seniority signals beyond title (advisor, board, kept-in-the-loop on big calls)

Signals that lower WIS:
- All execution, no strategy
- No mentorship or knowledge-transfer evidence

Anchors:
- 5: Pure executor, no strategy or mentorship.
- 10: Some mentorship, occasional strategy input.
- 15: Owns strategy for a function, mentors actively.
- 20: Recognized strategic voice, advises others outside their org.

---

## CHA (Influence)

What it measures: communication, leadership, ability to move people.

Signals that raise CHA:
- Public speaking (talks, podcasts, panels)
- Writing (blog, book, newsletter, op-eds)
- Sales numbers
- Cross-functional leadership
- Community building
- Portfolio polish (website, design quality, presentation)

Signals that lower CHA:
- Resume itself is poorly written or formatted
- No public-facing work
- No cross-team leadership

Anchors:
- 5: Heads-down IC with no public voice.
- 10: Some cross-functional work, internal influence.
- 15: External speaking or writing, recognized in a niche.
- 20: Public figure in domain, large following or audience, sells the vision.
